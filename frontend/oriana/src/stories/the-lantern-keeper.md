# Chapter One - Same First Name, Different Surname {-}

Possibilities and Randomness, something I've always interested in. Could you imagine it ? There were probably thoudsands of names, faces or lives that I could have came across LinkedIn that day. Each one of those lives, can simply disappear beneath the next with a simple momvement of my thumb. 

But then I saw hers. 
Nam Anh? That's my name ?

For a moment, it felt almost odd seeing it belong to someone else, especially a female in this case!? I read it twice, as if the second time would make it any less strange lah. 

Nam Anh Nguyen, was her name, and beneath the small absurdity of it, a thought of me lingered for days. 

I was Nam Anh Tran, she was Nam Anh Nguyen. Same first name, different surname. Two stranger, in the same city, carrying the name through completely seperate lives! I didn't know her, I didn't know her voice, the way she laughed, the things that kept her awake at night, of the light that she radiates. I knew nothing about her, at all!  

I could have kept scrolling and forget about it. Sometimes, I wonder what would have happened if I had. There are an entire version of our lives built from moments like that. A second earlier perhaps you would have made a different decisions! One small movement of the hand, and boom! someone who would one day mean everything remains nobody. 

But, I didn't. 

Something about seeing my own name attached to somebody else I had never met made me stop. At the time, it was genuine curiosity! Then a text was sent 

"Hey, js thought its funny that we almost had the same name" 

Maybe some stories begin long before we understand that they have started ? Maybe, fate is rarely as dramatic as poeple imagine it to be. Maybe sometimes, it looks exactly like my sister says, 

"Together, you'll be known as Male Nam Anh and Female Nam Anh".

And at that time, neither of us knew how much those two names were about to become entangled.

# Chapter Two - It's Fun to Meet You! {-}

"Hey, yes haha", her first message. 

Three words that should have belonged to an ordinary conversation between strangers. There was nothing romantic hidden inside her words, nothing that could have warned me what she would eventually become to me. At that moment, she was still only the girl whom happen to share my name, and I was only the boy who had been curious enough to reached out. 

Then, she told my something I would remember much more fondly later, she had "almost" messaged me first but "hesitated". 

There was something odd about knowing that now. Could it be that while I had been sitting on one side of the screen, wondering whether I should say something to this girl whom I knew absolute nothing about. She had apparently, been somewhere on the other side, wondering almost the same thing as me !? 

Perhaps, our beginning was closer than I realised, perhaps, If I had waited for a little while, our story would still have begun, except the first message would have belonged to my Ori instead of me :))) 

I would love that possibility. 

The conversation then continues with its own flow. She asked me if I was Vietnamese. I then answered in the strage half English, half Vietnamese way that had become natural to me, or perhaps my way of sparking the conversations. 

"Mắc cười kiểu nửa English nửa Vietnamese của you ghê"

She though my "Vinglish" was funny. 

And from there, somehow, the conversation simply, refused to end. We talked about University, our degrees, our internships, Melbourne itself, about my English, and Vietnamese capability which I am horrible at, talked about international schools, friends, languages... It was an endless list of topics. One subject became another, and another, until neither of us seemed particularly concerned with how far we had warndered from the reason we started talking in the first place, or was there any reason at all :))) 

With her, its different. There are people, well... mostly everyone I've encountered, these people are where you spoke with and had to constantly feel the need to search for the next sentence. You ask question because silence arrived and neither of you knows what else to do. Eventually, the replies become shorter and pause. 

With Ori, it was effortless. Every answer seemed to leave something behind for me to pick up, every little joke opened another door. I would learn something about her, ask another question, tease her about something, and suddenly another few minutes disappeared. 

The way she joked, the way she reassured someone she had only known for a few hours, the way her messages carried this strange warmth that made the screen between feel slightly less distant. Perhaps, that is how people begin entering our lives, a joke here, a question there, a little kindness neither person thinks much about at that time. Until suddenly, you know more about them than you did yesterdaym and for some reason, you want to know more about them the next day, and the next. 

Instagram, I didn't know then that asking for it would become the first step away from the coincidence that introduced us and toward something much more personal. 

Oriana, until then, she had been Nam Anh Nguyen to me. The girl from LinkedIn, the stranger I had messaged because seeing my own name attached to someone else's face had been too strange too ignore. 

But there she is, Oriana. The name I would eventually attach to memories instead of curiosity, a name that would no longer feel strange to me but what I called home. A name that would become hers in a way that "Nam Anh" could never be, because Nam Anh had always belonged to both of us. 

Oriana, belonged to her. 

Her humor, her uncertainty, her kindness, her voice, laughs, eyes, the way she would look infront of me, the way it would feel when this girl stopped being someone I knew through a phone and became whose pressence could change the atmosphere around me. At that time, none of that belonged to me yet. 

But, perhaps, this was the first moment she stopped feeling completely like a stranger, and perhaps, it was enough. 

Until sometime before midnight, I realised I was no longer talking to her because we happened to have the same name, but rather because I wanted to. And, for the first time, our names were no longer the most interesting, but rather the things we had in common!



# Chapter Three - Before I Met You {-}

We moved to Instagram that night.

And somehow, LinkedIn already felt far away.

The girl I had found through a name was no longer sitting between internships, university degrees and professional introductions. There were photos now. Stories. Small pieces of a life that had existed long before I had any place in it.

There was also another name.

**Oriana.**

This chapter will be shorter than the others.

Not because nothing happened here. Quite the opposite.

We talked. We learned things about each other. The conversations became easier, more frequent, more familiar. Slowly, the stranger behind the screen became someone whose name I started noticing a little faster whenever it appeared on my phone.

But some chapters are short not because they mattered less, but because something on the next page makes you impatient to turn it.

And I was impatient.

I could probably tell you every little thing we spoke about during those days. I could stretch these pages with messages, jokes, questions, late-night conversations and all the small details through which I slowly began to know her.

But even now, writing this, I find myself wanting to rush through them.

Because I know what comes next.

I get to meet her.

And perhaps that is the most truthful way to write this part of our story.

Because even then, I think I was beginning to feel the same way.

Messages were no longer enough.

Every conversation made me a little more curious about the person behind it. Every photograph made the distance between a screen and reality feel slightly more irritating. I knew what she looked like, technically, but photographs are still only frozen versions of someone. They cannot tell you how a person's expression changes when they see you. They cannot give you their voice, their movements, their nervousness, their presence.

I wanted all the things a screen couldn't give me.

I wanted to know what happened to her face just before she laughed. Whether she spoke with her hands. Whether there would be an awkward silence when we first stood in front of each other. Whether her voice sounded anything like the one I had unconsciously invented for her in my head.

And, maybe more than I wanted to admit, I wanted to know how she would look at me.

Until then, Oriana existed somewhere between imagination and reality.

Close enough for me to become curious.

Far enough for me to still wonder.

There are dozens of messages I could place here and tell you were important. Maybe one day I will. But I don't want to stay on Instagram for very long.

I want to turn the page.

I want to get to the part where the typing stops.

Where there is no profile picture between us.

No notification.

No little green light telling me she is online.

Just her.

And me.

For the first time.

So forgive me for rushing this chapter.

I was excited to meet you.
